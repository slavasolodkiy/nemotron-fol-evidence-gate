NEMOTRON × EVIDENCE GATE.

A local multimodal model read the passport. Then the deterministic layer refused to believe it. Can one local model be the perception layer of an evidence-gated compliance agent — without becoming the final authority?

01

Test environment

TIGHT LOCAL FIT

▚ HARDWARE

Olares One · NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 Laptop GPU · 24,463 MiB VRAM · ~96 GB installed RAM (93 GiB visible).

▚ RUNTIME

Ollama 0.30.11 in Olares / Kubernetes · Nemotron 33b-q4_K_M · payload ~27.6 GB · OpenAI-style local HTTP workflow.

▚ FORMAL LAYER

Python deterministic validator · ICAO-style MRZ length/charset · check digits + cross-field comparisons · SWI-Prolog policy engine.

▚ WHY NEMOTRON / WHY WE REFUSED TO TRUST IT

One local model for text + vision keeps sensitive evidence on-device. But in regulated workflows a plausible answer is not evidence — the experiment was designed so a deterministic layer could reject a confident but malformed extraction.

02

Architecture — four layers, four jobs

AUTHORITY SEPARATION
01 · PERCEPTIONNEMOTRON

“What does the image appear to contain?”

Extract human-readable fields + candidate MRZ strings from a synthetic passport image.

02 · VERIFICATIONPYTHON / ICAO

“Does the extraction survive deterministic checks?”

Length, charset, MRZ check digits, cross-field consistency, uncertainty sanity.

03 · DECISIONPROLOG / FOL

“Given verified facts, what policy action follows?”

The validator exports facts; the policy engine reasons over facts — not over model prose.

04 · EXPLANATIONNEMOTRON

“Tell the reviewer what happened.”

Explicitly forbidden from changing, softening or reversing the decision.

03

T1 — clean synthetic passport

HIGH-CONTRAST · KNOWN GROUND TRUTH

▚ WHAT WORKED

9/9 human-readable fields extracted correctly. The model separated the printed passport number from the MRZ check digit. Structured JSON via Ollama API.

▚ WHAT FAILED

MRZ was not reproduced exactly — characters added/dropped in machine-readable lines — while reporting uncertain_fields: [].

07

What this actually proves

HONEST BOUNDARIES

▚ PROVEN IN THIS PILOT

Nemotron 3 Nano Omni Q4_K_M runs locally on the Olares One · 24GB RTX 5090 hosts most of the model · vision extraction works via local API · human-readable fields extracted accurately on simple synthetic specimens · deterministic MRZ/cross-field checks catch model errors · Prolog deterministically refuses automated acceptance · the model explains the policy result without changing it.

▚ NOT PROVEN

Production-grade OCR across real passports · reliable uncertainty calibration · legal/regulatory compliance · document authenticity, fraud, liveness or biometrics · that Prolog rules faithfully encode real law · audio/video paths production-ready · anything involving real customer PII — none was used.

▚ WHY IT MATTERS · PRIVACY

Local multimodal perception keeps sensitive evidence on-device while exposing a structured interface to downstream controls.

▚ WHY IT MATTERS · AUDITABILITY

Checksums, structure rules and explicit conflicts leave a trace that is reproducible without trusting model confidence.

Notes & caveats. Home-lab engineering experiment, not a benchmark paper and not a compliance certification. Synthetic passport data only. The deterministic checks validate properties of extracted data; they do not prove a document is genuine or a person is who they claim to be. Next: audio/video evidence → gate; realistic specimen scans; a versioned policy library; perception, verification, logic and explanation as separate components — not one trusted black box.
